Caffeine & Sleep Architecture

Under 25 mg (Target): Safe baseline. Ensures adenosine receptors in the brain are unblocked, allowing natural deep slow-wave sleep.

50 mg: Mild residual caffeine. Safe for most habitual drinkers, though sensitive individuals may experience slight latency in falling asleep.

Above 100 mg: Significantly delays sleep onset, suppresses REM sleep, and increases nighttime awakenings.
